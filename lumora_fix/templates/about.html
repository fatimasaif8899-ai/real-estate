About LUMORA

A playful digital reading room where old stories, soft colours and curious minds meet.

A library without walls

Made for slow discovery and happy little detours.

LUMORA brings together public-domain literature and a gentle set of tools for modern readers. Search the archive, open a book, save favourites, organise collections and keep your reading journey close.

The visual world is inspired by illustrated children’s books, anime stationery, whimsical editorial pages, botanical stickers and playful bookshelf corners.
