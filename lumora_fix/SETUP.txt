VIDEOS
- Put hero-library-anime.mp4 and about-reading-anime.mp4 in static/uploads/illustrations, OR upload them from Admin > Settings.
- Supported: MP4, WEBM, OGG, MOV.
- The app scans the folder, so the video does not depend on one exact filename.

BOOK COVERS
- Admin > Library > Add Book/Edit Book has a real cover-file upload.
- Uploaded covers go to static/uploads/covers.

FOOTER
- Footer appears only on Home, Library, Collections, About and Contact.
- Login/Register, Dashboard and Admin pages have no footer.

DESIGN
- Playful anime/stationery/illustrated-book style.
- Pastel colour combinations, doodles, rounded cards, playful sidebars and mixed display fonts.
- Categories include children’s stories, Urdu novels, English novels, romance, mystery, fantasy, adventure, poetry, classics, philosophy, horror/gothic, YA, history, science and self growth.

VIDEO NAMES
- For the Home hero, use a filename beginning with "hero", e.g. hero-library-anime.mp4.
- For the About page, use a filename beginning with "about", e.g. about-reading-anime.mp4.
- If only one video exists, LUMORA uses it as a fallback in both locations.
- MP4 (H.264/AAC) is recommended for Chrome/Edge/Firefox. WEBM is also supported.
