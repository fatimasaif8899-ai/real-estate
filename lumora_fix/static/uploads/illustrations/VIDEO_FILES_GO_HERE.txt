LUMORA VIDEO SETUP

Hero video:
- Put one or more videos here whose filename contains "hero".
- Recommended: hero-library.mp4 (H.264/AAC, 16:9 or 1920x1080).

About video:
- Put one or more videos here whose filename contains "about".
- Recommended: about-library.mp4 (H.264/AAC, portrait or 4:5).

The website automatically scans this folder. You do NOT need to edit HTML.
MP4 and WEBM are recommended for browser compatibility. MOV is accepted but may not play in every browser.
You can also upload the two videos from Admin -> Settings.
